About the MAPS Center

Build what matters. Build it well. Build it together.

The MAPS Center for Entrepreneurship & Innovation is being developed as a year-round home for Muslim builders in Greater Seattle. It brings together the community, practical learning, disciplined venture-building, ethical reflection, and pathways to contribute back.

Our mission

We help spiritually grounded builders develop the skills, relationships, and disciplined execution needed to turn meaningful problems into useful ventures and institutions.

What we believe

Ambition needs a moral direction

Building profitable and enduring organizations can serve the public good. The question is not whether ambition is good or bad; it is what the ambition serves, who benefits, who bears the risk, and what kind of future it creates.

Evidence is a form of respect

Builders should understand the people affected by a problem before prescribing a solution. We prioritize direct conversations, observation, experiments, working artifacts, and honest learning.

Community should produce capability

Belonging matters, but a builder community should also make its members more useful, more courageous, more disciplined, and better able to help others.

Success carries responsibility

Knowledge, access, wealth, and influence become more meaningful when they expand opportunity and strengthen the institutions that serve people.

Who this is for

The center is designed first for active Muslim builders and early-stage founders in Greater Seattle: founders, engineers, product leaders, researchers, designers, creatives, operators, students, and professionals prepared to contribute to real work.

Programs may welcome participants and collaborators of other backgrounds who respect the center’s Islamic ethical grounding and community standards.

What the center is—and is not

The center isThe center is not
A year-round builder communityOnly a networking group
A place for practical learning and accountable workA calendar of motivational talks
A pathway from problem discovery to tested ventureA pitch competition factory
Ethically and spiritually groundedA label placed on ordinary startup culture
Connected to external partners and next-stage opportunitiesA guarantee of funding or commercial success
Designed to strengthen the wider communityAn exclusive club that exists for status

Governance and transparency

The center is currently being developed with MAPS stakeholders and members of Greater Seattle’s technology, entrepreneurial, and community ecosystem. Governance, program ownership, facility access, budgets, safety, and reporting will be finalized before programs are represented as operational.
